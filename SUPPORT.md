# NovaCart Theme — Support Policy

## Contact

For help with your purchase, contact the author through the **support / messaging channel of the marketplace or sales site where you bought NovaCart**.

Please include:
- **NovaCart version 1.6.7**
- A short description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce
- A cropped screenshot when useful

The author aims to acknowledge support requests within **two business days** and will give a next-step estimate when more investigation is needed. Response time may vary on weekends and public holidays.

## Support scope

Support covers:
- Installation of the included theme ZIP on Shopify
- Documented theme settings and sections
- Reproducible defects in the **unmodified** theme

Outside normal support scope:
- Custom development for a specific store
- Third-party app behavior
- Merchant product data / content
- Payment, shipping, or Shopify account / checkout issues

We can still help identify whether the theme is involved.

## Protect your store

Do not send passwords, one-time codes, payment details, customer personal data, or unrestricted staff access. Prefer a development theme or development store, and share only what is needed to reproduce the issue.

## Storefront customers

The theme author supports **buyers of this theme**. Shoppers on a merchant’s live store should contact that merchant for orders, delivery, returns, and payments. Merchants should configure their own contact and policy pages in Shopify.
